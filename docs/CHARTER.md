# Charter — w10-tope

> The constitution of this product. When my agent and I disagree about a feature at 2am, this is the referee. Written BEFORE the first line of code.

**What it exists to do:** answer one question, only when asked: *"¿Me alcanza esta cuota, según MI regla?"* It adds every installment the person already owes (Kueski, Aplazo, Mercado Pago, MSI, Coppel…) against his quincena and quotes his own rule back to him.

**For whom:** the online installment buyer, 22–38, with 2+ BNPL or MSI lines, *after* the first bad month — scared enough to write a rule on a calm Sunday. Not the fiado household: Don Pepe already is their guardrail.

**What it will never do:**
1. Offer, show or link credit of any kind (no "consolida tu deuda", no "pre-aprobado").
2. Start a conversation: no notifications, no reminders, no tips, no product shown.
3. Write, add or change a rule for him; score him; count his "no"s.
4. Store his screenshots, his debts or his rule on a server. Everything lives on his phone and is deletable in one tap.
5. Say "yes" when it isn't sure. Uncertain = "No sé, revisa".
6. Be paid per sale, per loan or per referral.

**Long view (3 sentences):** If this slice works, Tope becomes the buyer-side ledger that Mexico's open-finance law (Ley Fintech art. 76) promised and never delivered: one place where all of a person's cuotas add up, owned by him, with no lender in the room. In three years, when buyer-side agents arrive, Tope is the rulebook those agents must obey — the agent proposes, the person's own rule disposes. It stays paid like a utility (same price for yes and no), never like a marketplace.
